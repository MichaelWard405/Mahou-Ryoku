the magic within Mahou Ryoku is gained through multiple means all of which use an array of [[Manifestation]] methods these methods will determine what [[Spells]] your capable of using, and to further add to that your Mahou itself will contain a [[Affinity]] these determine what "elements" of magic you'll be capable of using.

Your [[Manifestation]] and [[Affinity]] are determined by either your [[Race]] or through certain actions you can [[Attune]] your self with different [[Affinity]] and through [[Wisdom]] alternate [[Manifestation]] methods can be learnt.

most [[Spells]] requires a cost of mana or as its called in Mahou Ryoku, [[Mahou]] there are two ways of [[Mahou Storage]], first is the most common being with a [[Mahou Pool]] which is an internal storage of mahou within the body that is called fourth using [[Internal Manifestation]], the second being [[Crystalline Mahou]] a Crystallised form of [[Mahou]] that can be found growing in [[Mahou Dense Regions]] and these [[Crystalline Mahou]] will take on the [[Environmental Affinity]], and they have a [[Purity]] which will determine the amount of [[Mahou]] they contain for spell casting and there effectiveness in recipes and [[Inscriptions]] 

there do exist [[Spells]] that reside outside the traditional bounds of [[Mahou]] these often require a cost that isn't [[Mahou]] key examples of these are the [[Ritual]] [[Manifestation]] type as there exist [[Ritual]]'s that don't require [[Mahou]] to use allowing even those without [[Mahou]] capability to use magic or even [[Attune]] themselves to gain a [[Mahou Pool]], and [[Affinity]] example of this would be the [[Celestial]] [[Affinity]] as it uses energies gained from various [[Celestial]] beings to [Manifest](Manifestation) [[Celestial]] [[Spells]] or even mimic [[Spells]] of other [Affinities](Affinity.md) or [Races](Race)
